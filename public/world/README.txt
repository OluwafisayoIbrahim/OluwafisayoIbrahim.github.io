Country and city data derived from GeoNames cities500, countryInfo and admin1CodesASCII.
Source: https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/

GeoNames data is supplied as is, without guarantees of completeness or current accuracy.
Downloaded for this portfolio on 2026-10-31. Country and city JSON was reduced and reorganised by country.
Includes 250 current countries and territories (including Kosovo); obsolete AN and CS entries were excluded.
Includes 236857 populated-place records. Four territories have no cities in this extract.
Geographic data supports the city selectors, clock and globe. Cultural stories are stored separately with individual source links.
Elevation may be the GeoNames DEM terrain estimate. Distance to the equator is an approximation.
Population is retained as source metadata for ordering, not displayed as a live estimate.
